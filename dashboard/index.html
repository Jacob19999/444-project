The cleaned CSV is split into a snowflake schema. fact_admission holds one row per admission with its measures. Each dimension table describes one side of it. Two dimensions are normalized one more level: patient links to gender and blood type, and date links to month. That second level is what makes it a snowflake rather than a star.

Data: Kaggle “Healthcare Dataset” by prasad22. It is synthetic, so patterns describe this dataset and are not clinical findings.

Built from the snowflake-schema tables in output/tables/. The 534 duplicate rows are removed. The 106 negative bills are flagged as adjustments and left out of cost figures unless you include them with the filter above.
